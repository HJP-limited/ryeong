Preferred real-device test flow: do not package large models in the APK.
Push the model after install to the app-specific external files directory:

/sdcard/Android/data/com.example.hjp/files/models/functiongemma_270m.litertlm

The app also supports packaging the FunctionGemma 270M LiteRT-LM model here as:

functiongemma_270m.litertlm

or placing it in the private runtime files directory:

files/models/functiongemma_270m.litertlm

Large .litertlm files are intentionally ignored by git.
